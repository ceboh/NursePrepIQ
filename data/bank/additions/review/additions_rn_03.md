### RN-A03-001 · Multiple choice · Management of Care

**Topic:** Prioritization: orthopedic unit

**Stem:** The nurse on an orthopedic unit receives report on four clients. Which client should the nurse assess first?

- A. A client 2 days after a total knee replacement who rates pain 4/10 during physical therapy
- B. A client in skeletal traction who asks for help repositioning in bed for comfort
- C. A client with a hip fracture awaiting surgery who is asking for a meal tray
- **✓ D. A client with a new forearm cast who has severe pain on passive finger movement despite an opioid**

**Answer:** D

**Rationale:** Severe pain that is out of proportion to the injury, worse with passive stretch, and unrelieved by opioids suggests acute compartment syndrome, which can cause permanent muscle and nerve damage within hours. Moderate pain during therapy, a repositioning request, and a client who should stay NPO before surgery can be addressed next.

### RN-A03-005 · Multiple choice · Management of Care

**Topic:** Patient Self-Determination Act

**Stem:** A client is being admitted to the hospital. Which action is required by the federal Patient Self-Determination Act?

- A. Requiring the client to complete an advance directive before admission can be finished
- **✓ B. Asking whether the client has an advance directive and informing them of their right to make one**
- C. Asking the client's family to decide on the client's code status during the admission
- D. Recommending that the client choose do-not-resuscitate status at the time of admission

**Answer:** B

**Rationale:** The Patient Self-Determination Act requires facilities that receive Medicare or Medicaid funding to ask clients on admission whether they have an advance directive, document it, and inform them of their right to make health care decisions and create one. Facilities may not require an advance directive or base care on whether one exists, and code status is the client's decision, not the family's or the staff's.

### RN-A03-006 · Multiple choice · Management of Care

**Topic:** Good Samaritan laws

**Stem:** An off-duty nurse at a youth soccer game starts CPR on a spectator who collapsed and is not breathing. Which statement about the nurse's legal protection is accurate?

- A. The nurse is legally protected only if the person signs a consent form after recovering from the event
- B. Good Samaritan laws also protect care that is grossly negligent
- **✓ C. Good Samaritan laws generally protect a nurse who gives reasonable emergency care without pay**
- D. The nurse must accept payment to be protected

**Answer:** C

**Rationale:** Good Samaritan laws vary by state but generally protect people who give emergency care in good faith, without compensation, and within their training. Consent is implied when a person is unresponsive, protection does not cover gross negligence, and accepting payment usually removes the protection.

### RN-A03-007 · Multiple choice · Management of Care

**Topic:** Legal scope of nursing practice

**Stem:** A nurse is unsure whether a task is within the RN scope of practice in their state. Which source defines the legal scope of nursing practice?

- A. The hospital's job description for RNs who work on the nurse's assigned unit
- B. The American Nurses Association's code of ethics for all nurses who practice
- C. The provider who writes the prescription
- **✓ D. The state's nurse practice act, as administered by the board of nursing**

**Answer:** D

**Rationale:** Each state's nurse practice act is the law that defines nursing scope of practice, and the state board of nursing interprets and enforces it. Facility policies can be more restrictive but cannot expand scope beyond the law, the ANA code of ethics guides ethical conduct rather than legal scope, and a provider cannot authorize a nurse to act outside it.

### RN-A03-008 · Multiple choice · Management of Care

**Topic:** Principle of double effect

**Stem:** A client who is dying has severe dyspnea. The family worries that giving the prescribed morphine will hasten death. Which ethical principle supports giving the morphine as prescribed?

- **✓ A. Double effect**
- B. Distributive justice
- C. Veracity and truth-telling
- D. Paternalism toward the client

**Answer:** A

**Rationale:** The principle of double effect holds that an action intended to produce a good effect, such as relieving dyspnea, is ethical even if a possible harmful effect is foreseen but not intended, as long as the good outweighs the risk. Opioids carefully titrated for dyspnea at the end of life have not been shown to shorten life significantly. Distributive justice concerns fair allocation of resources, veracity concerns truthfulness, and paternalism overrides a client's choices.

### RN-A03-009 · Multiple choice · Management of Care

**Topic:** Respecting a client's choice of decision-maker

**Stem:** An older client from a culture in which families make medical decisions together asks the nurse to discuss the diagnosis with their eldest son instead of with them. What should the nurse do?

- A. Insist on telling the client directly, since adults must receive their own diagnoses
- **✓ B. Respect the client's wish and confirm who will receive information and make decisions**
- C. Tell the son everything and stop including the client in all of the discussions
- D. Explain that the provider will decide who receives the client's information

**Answer:** B

**Rationale:** Autonomy includes a client's right to choose not to receive information personally and to have a trusted person receive it and help decide. The nurse confirms and documents the client's preference, involves the provider, and checks periodically whether the client wants to be included. Forcing the information on the client, excluding the client entirely, or letting the provider choose does not respect the client's wishes.

### RN-A03-010 · Multiple choice · Management of Care

**Topic:** Disclosure of errors

**Stem:** A client received a medication dose twice because of a documentation error and developed hypotension that required treatment. Which statement about telling the client is accurate?

- A. The client should not be told, since the hypotension resolved and no lasting harm has resulted from it
- B. Only the client's family should be told
- **✓ C. The client should be told about the error promptly, with an explanation and an apology**
- D. The client should be told only if they ask

**Answer:** C

**Rationale:** Clients have a right to know about errors that reach them, especially those that cause harm. Disclosure is prompt and honest, includes what happened, what is being done, and an apology, and follows facility policy, usually led by the provider or a leader with the nurse involved. Resolution of the harm, telling only family, and waiting for the client to ask do not meet the standard of transparency.

### RN-A03-020 · Drop-down cloze · Management of Care

**Topic:** Just culture

**Stem:** Complete the sentence by choosing from the lists of options.

- Blank 1: **✓ supported while the system is examined** / disciplined to deter future errors / dismissed
- Blank 2: **✓ held accountable through remedial or disciplinary action** / treated the same as a nurse who made an honest mistake / excused because errors are always system problems

**Answer:** 1: supported while the system is examined; 2: held accountable through remedial or disciplinary action

### RN-A03-023 · Multiple choice · Basic Care and Comfort

**Topic:** Walking with a cane

**Stem:** The nurse is reinforcing teaching for a client with right knee weakness who has a new single-point cane. Which instruction is correct?

- **✓ A. Hold the cane in the left hand and move it forward with the right leg**
- B. Hold the cane in the right hand and move it forward together with the right leg each time
- C. Move the cane forward after both legs have stepped
- D. Use the cane on whichever side feels best

**Answer:** A

**Rationale:** A cane is held on the stronger side and advanced with the weaker leg, which widens the base of support and shifts weight away from the weak leg. Holding it on the weak side, moving it after both legs, or changing sides reduces stability.
