## PN-C31: Opioid-induced respiratory depression after surgery

System: Respiratory · Discipline: Pharmacology

**Scenario:** A 72-year-old client with obstructive sleep apnea had a laparoscopic cholecystectomy this morning. At 2200, the client received oxycodone 10 mg by mouth for pain, and at 2230, lorazepam 0.5 mg by mouth for anxiety. At 2330, the assistive personnel reports that the client is hard to wake.

**Nurses' notes, 2330:** Arouses briefly to a loud voice and a sternal rub, then falls back asleep. Snoring, with pauses in breathing of about 15 seconds. Pupils pinpoint. Not wearing the home CPAP. Pain was 6/10 before the oxycodone dose.

**Vital signs, 2330:** T 98.4°F (36.9°C), HR 62/min, RR 8/min, BP 104/62 mm Hg, SpO2 85% on room air.

**Prescriptions:** Oxycodone 5 to 10 mg by mouth every 4 hours as needed for pain. Lorazepam 0.5 mg by mouth every 8 hours as needed for anxiety. Naloxone per protocol for RR below 10/min or severe sedation. CPAP at night at home settings. Opioid sedation scale every 2 hours for the first 24 hours.

**Rationale:** Severe sedation, apneic pauses, pinpoint pupils, a respiratory rate of 8/min, and hypoxemia after an opioid combined with a benzodiazepine, in a client with sleep apnea who is not using CPAP, indicate opioid-induced respiratory depression. The temperature is normal.

## PN-C33: Methotrexate toxicity in rheumatoid arthritis

**Vital signs:** T 101.6°F (38.7°C), HR 104/min, RR 22/min, BP 118/72 mm Hg, SpO2 93% on room air.

**Laboratory results:** WBC 2,100/mm3 (5,000–10,000). Absolute neutrophil count 900/mm3. Platelets 88,000/mm3 (150,000–400,000). ALT 96 units/L (7–56).

**Medication list:** Methotrexate 15 mg by mouth once weekly. Folic acid 1 mg by mouth daily. Prednisone 5 mg by mouth daily.
