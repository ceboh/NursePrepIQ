# Validation Review — Pilot Batches 0032 and 0033

Date: 2026-09-23
Reviewer: GPT-5.6 Sol (AI-assisted review; not human review and not psychometric validation)
Scope: 200 current pilot items (100 in migration 0032; 100 in migration 0033).

## Decision

No item is promoted or published by this review. All 200 remain lifecycle_status=pilot and validation_status=pilot.

## Clinical review

The 50 base clinical scenarios (25 per batch, each expanded into four RN/PN variants) were reviewed for the keyed clinical action, safety priority, distractor safety, rationale consistency, and role framing.

196/200 variants receive an AI-assisted clinical pass.

The four C. difficile variants in batch 0032 require revision before clinical pass. The keyed response states that soap-and-water hand hygiene should be used after care as a general rule. Current CDC healthcare-worker guidance prefers alcohol-based hand sanitizer in most clinical situations, including routine care of patients with C. difficile when hands are not visibly soiled; soap and water is specifically encouraged during C. difficile outbreaks and is required when hands are visibly soiled. The item should be rewritten to avoid teaching an overbroad rule.

## NCLEX / clinical-judgment alignment review

The batch generator rotates the six NCJMM functions, but every variant retains action-oriented answer options. That works reasonably for Generate Solutions and Take Action, but creates a construct mismatch for Recognize Cues, Analyze Cues, Prioritize Hypotheses, and Evaluate Outcomes.

Per 100-item batch:
- Generate Solutions: 17 — alignment pass
- Take Action: 16 — alignment pass
- Recognize Cues: 17 — needs review
- Analyze Cues: 17 — needs review
- Prioritize Hypotheses: 17 — needs review
- Evaluate Outcomes: 16 — needs review

Across both batches: 66 alignment passes and 134 alignment needs-review outcomes. The affected variants must have answer choices rewritten so they actually measure the named function rather than merely relabeling an intervention question.

## Editorial/adversarial review

The same construct mismatch is an editorial/adversarial defect because the stem asks for a cue, interpretation, hypothesis, or outcome while the choices are actions. Those 134 variants receive editorial needs_review. The 66 Generate Solutions / Take Action variants receive editorial pass, subject to the C. difficile clinical correction.

Answer positions remain deliberately balanced by construction. No human-review, pilot-performance, discrimination, or psychometric claim is created.

## Authoritative anchors

- NCSBN 2026 NCLEX-RN and NCLEX-PN Test Plans and clinical-judgment framework.
- CDC healthcare hand-hygiene guidance for C. difficile.
- CDC transmission-based precautions and tuberculosis infection-control guidance.
- Current standard emergency nursing principles reflected in the scenarios; medication/lab/threshold-specific items remain subject to human clinical review before trusted publication.

## Required remediation

1. Rewrite the 134 construct-mismatched variants so answer choices correspond to their assigned NCJMM function.
2. Correct the four C. difficile variants to match current CDC healthcare hand-hygiene guidance.
3. Re-run structural/editorial audit after remediation.
4. Keep all remediated items pilot until review events support the required gates. Do not fabricate human or psychometric validation.
