# NursePrepIQ Question Quality Standard

All generated clinical content enters PILOT only. Nothing in this document authorizes automatic production publication.

## Reasoning standard

Questions should measure entry-level nursing judgment rather than trivia. Prefer realistic clinical context with relevant and irrelevant cues, trends, competing priorities, medication/laboratory/assessment data when appropriate, and plausible distractors based on common misconceptions.

Every item must map to one NCJMM step: Recognize Cues, Analyze Cues, Prioritize Hypotheses, Generate Solutions, Take Action, or Evaluate Outcomes.

For batches of 10 or more pilot items, target at least 30% hard/high-discrimination items and no more than 25% easy items. Difficulty means reasoning demand, not obscure facts.

## Single-best-answer quality

Use four plausible options. Exactly one is best. Do not make the key obvious through length, grammar, absolutes, or three absurd distractors. The learner should need to discriminate between clinically plausible choices. Correct-answer positions must not become predictable; no position should exceed 40% in a batch of eight or more SBA items.

## NGN diversity

Production planning must include stand-alone clinical-judgment items and unfolding case-study work across all six NCJMM steps. Supported pipeline targets include multiple response, matrix/grid, cloze/dropdown, ordered response and bow-tie in addition to SBA. Item-format share is a product target, not a claim about a fixed NCLEX format percentage.

## RN/PN distinction

RN and PN items must test the appropriate role rather than merely changing the label in the stem. RN items may emphasize comprehensive assessment, synthesis, prioritization, planning and coordination. PN items should reflect practical/vocational nursing responsibilities such as focused data collection, implementation/reinforcement of established plans, monitoring and escalation, subject to jurisdiction-neutral entry-level framing.

## Rationales

Explain why the keyed response is best and why distractors are weaker in the presented situation. Avoid generic rationales. Rationales must not invent patient facts absent from the scenario.

## Validation gates

A question remains pilot until it passes schema, clinical, NCLEX-alignment, editorial and pilot gates. Generated count, pilot count, validated count and production-active count remain separate metrics.

## Authoritative alignment

Use the effective 2026 NCLEX-RN and NCLEX-PN Test Plans and NCSBN clinical-judgment guidance for blueprint and clinical-judgment alignment. NursePrepIQ items must be original; do not copy, reconstruct, or claim access to secure NCLEX items.
